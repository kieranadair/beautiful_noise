### What is Beautiful Noise?

A living, community-driven archive of gig posters from Naarm/Melbourne's live music scene. Every week the city's streets fill with bold, strange, beautiful posters — then they're torn down, pasted over, and lost. This is a place to pull that art off the street (and away from the mercy of the algorithm) and preserve it, so the memory of the scene endures for everyone who comes after.

### What can I upload?

Genuine gig posters and related promotional artwork from Naarm's live music scene. Please only upload posters you have the right to share, or that you're preserving in good faith for their historical and cultural value. Skip anything that isn't a real gig poster, anything unrelated to live music, and anything containing someone's private information.

### How do I upload a poster?

Head to **Upload**, drop in an image or PDF, and our AI reads the poster for you — pulling out the headliners, support acts, date, venue, and any event name. You review those suggestions, fix anything it got wrong, add who made the poster if you know, and hit save. Nothing goes into the archive until *you* confirm it — the AI only ever makes suggestions.

### The AI got something wrong. What do I do?

Before you save, just edit it — every field on the review form is yours to change, and you can add or remove bands, swap the venue, or fix the date on the spot. If you spot a mistake on a poster that's *already* in the archive, use the [Contact](/contact_page) page to submit a correction — an admin reviews every request before anything changes.

### What does "Poster By" mean?

It's credit for the people who *made* the poster — designers, photographers, illustrators, anyone whose hands shaped the artwork (not the bands playing the show). If you know who made a poster, adding them here makes sure they get the recognition they deserve. Leave it blank if you're not sure.

### What is a community upload?

Sometimes a poster is too good to lose to the algorithm, even when we can't reach the person who made it. Community uploads let anyone rescue that piece of history — they're posters shared by members of the community for their cultural value, rather than by the rights holder directly. They carry a small :material/groups: marker so you know the creator hasn't personally signed off. And if that creator ever turns up, they can claim or remove their work at any time.

### I made a poster that someone else uploaded — can I claim or remove it?

Yes, always. Head to [Contact](/contact_page), find your poster, and either **authorise** it (to remove the community-upload flag and have it stand as officially shared) or **request its removal**. Your call — this is your work.

### Who owns the posters? Can I reuse them?

Creators keep every ounce of their copyright — uploading to Beautiful Noise doesn't sign any of it away. Archive content is shared under **Creative Commons BY-NC 4.0**: you're free to share and reference it *with credit*, but not for commercial use.

### How do I get something corrected or taken down?

Everything runs through the [Contact](/contact_page) page. You can:
- Correct a band, venue, poster credit, or event name
- Fix a poster's date
- Adjust a lineup (add/remove bands, change headliner vs support)
- Authorise a community upload as the rights holder
- Request a takedown

Pick your poster, choose what you need, and submit — every request is reviewed by a human before any change is made.

### Do I need an account to use Beautiful Noise?

No. Browsing and uploading are both open and anonymous — no sign-up, no login. Just bring a poster.

### Why only Naarm/Melbourne?

Because this scene is worth preserving on its own terms. Beautiful Noise is a love letter to the bands, artists, and venues that make Naarm's live music what it is — start narrow, do it justice.
